Privacy policy

Gmail-API-Access-Unix-Mail-stack. Last updated: 28 October 2026.

This policy describes how the personal Gmail integration named Gmail-API-Access-Unix-Mail-stack, operated by Mads Petersen, accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data. The integration is used only for the owner's own Gmail account and correspondence. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Google user data accessed

The integration requests the Gmail scope https://mail.google.com/, which is required for IMAP and SMTP access. Through it, the integration accesses email headers, message bodies and attachments in the owner's Gmail account, and can send messages from that account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the owner with these features: synchronizing mail to a local mailbox, searching and organizing correspondence, summarizing messages, preparing drafts, and sending messages when instructed by the owner. In the job-search workflow, outgoing messages to employers require the owner's approval. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used for any purpose unrelated to these features.

Storage and protection

Mail copies, search indexes and selected job-search records are stored on the owner's own computer. OAuth credentials are stored separately in local files readable only by the owner's account. Connections to Google use encrypted IMAP and SMTP (TLS).

Sharing and AI processing

Relevant message content may be sent to AI services configured in the owner's OpenClaw system, currently OpenAI (ChatGPT/Codex) and Anthropic (Claude), to summarize correspondence or prepare drafts for the owner. Only the content needed for the task requested by the owner is sent. Google user data is not shared with any other third party, and no person other than the owner reads it.

Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Limited Use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local copies and records remain until the owner removes them. Deleting a local record does not by itself delete a message from Gmail. The owner can revoke the integration's access at any time in Google Account permissions, and can delete the stored credentials, local mail copies and records from the owner's computer.
