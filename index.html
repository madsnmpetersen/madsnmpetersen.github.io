Gmail-API-Access-Unix-Mail-stack

Gmail-API-Access-Unix-Mail-stack is a private mail integration operated by Mads Petersen for his own Gmail account, used for personal job-search correspondence. It is not a public email service, has no other users and does not offer public registration.

What the app does

The app signs in to the owner's Gmail account with Google OAuth and uses Gmail IMAP and SMTP to:

  • synchronize the owner's mail to a local mailbox on the owner's own computer;
  • search and organize that correspondence;
  • prepare draft replies, and send messages only when the owner instructs it to.

JobOps, the owner's personal job-search assistant, uses the app to organize applications and understand employer replies.

Privacy

How the app accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data is described in the privacy policy.
